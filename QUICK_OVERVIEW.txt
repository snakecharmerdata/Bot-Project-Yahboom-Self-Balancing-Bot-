==============================================
  OUR SELF BALANCING ROBOT: THE SIMPLE VERSION
==============================================

WHAT IS IT?
-----------
A robot on two wheels that balances itself, like a Segway.
A small computer (a Raspberry Pi) acts as its brain.
On the Pi's screen we can:

  - see what the robot sees (camera)
  - see what is around it (a "radar" map)
  - drive it with the keyboard
  - let it follow a face, an object or a hand signal


THE FOUR PARTS
--------------
1. THE ROBOT
   Two wheels, a motor and a battery. It keeps itself standing up.

2. THE CAMERA
   A tiny camera on the robot. It sends pictures to the Pi over WiFi.

3. THE LIDAR
   A small spinning sensor on top. It uses a laser to measure how far
   away things are, all the way around, many times a second.
   (Like a bat using sound, but with light.)

4. THE RASPBERRY PI
   A small computer. It runs our app, shows everything on screen,
   and tells the robot where to go.


HOW DO THEY TALK? (NO WIRES!)
-----------------------------

   Pi  --- Bluetooth --->  Robot        "go forward", "turn left", "stop"
   Camera --- WiFi --->  Pi             pictures
   LiDAR --> mini board --- WiFi --->  Pi    distances

  * Bluetooth is how the Pi sends driving orders. It's the same thing
    your phone uses for wireless earbuds.
  * WiFi carries the pictures and the distance readings.
  * The LiDAR can't do WiFi on its own, so we added a tiny board
    called an ESP32 (about the size of a stick of gum). It reads the
    LiDAR through a few wires and passes the data on over WiFi.
  * A power bank (the kind you charge your phone with) powers the
    ESP32 and the LiDAR.


WHAT WE ACTUALLY DID
--------------------
  1. Installed the app on the Raspberry Pi.
  2. Told the camera the name of our WiFi network.
  3. Paired the Pi with the robot over Bluetooth.
  4. Wired the LiDAR to the ESP32 board (4 wires: 2 for power,
     2 for data).
  5. Put a small program on the ESP32 that sends the LiDAR data
     over WiFi.
  6. Told the app where to find the ESP32 on the network.
  7. Opened the app, and everything showed up!


WHAT THE APP CAN DO
-------------------
  Camera tab ..... live video from the robot
  Drive tab ...... drive with buttons or arrow keys
  LiDAR tab ...... a map of everything around the robot
  Autopilot tab .. the robot follows a face, an object or your hand


SAFETY: THE ROBOT IS SMART ABOUT STOPPING
-----------------------------------------
  * Let go of the key and the robot stops.
  * Press SPACE or ESC to stop at any time.
  * If the LiDAR sees something closer than 35 cm in front,
    the robot refuses to drive into it.
  * If the connection is lost, the robot stops by itself.
  * Don't pick the robot up while it's balancing: the wheels
    will spin fast!


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
  * Turning the ESP32 off is fine. It remembers its program
    and starts again by itself when you turn it back on.
  * Keep the power bank charged. When it gets low, things act weird.
  * Some words you'll hear:
      WiFi / Bluetooth ... two kinds of wireless connection
      ESP32 .............. a tiny, cheap computer board with WiFi
      LiDAR .............. a laser distance sensor that spins
      ROS 2 .............. software robots use to share information
                           (our camera uses it to send pictures)
      Raspberry Pi ....... a small computer the size of a deck of cards
